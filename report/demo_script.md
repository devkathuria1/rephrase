# Rephrase: 5-minute demo script

> Content note: the demo uses mild insults, a threat and a hateful statement about a group (no slurs). Say this aloud before starting.

Setup before presenting: `streamlit run app.py`, provider = Groq (`openai/gpt-oss-120b`), models already loaded (do one throwaway analysis first). Have one screenshot ready (a chat bubble with the "idiot" sentence) and the Limitations page open in a second tab.

## 0:00 - 0:40 Intro
- Problem: people write things in anger that they would not say calmly; moderation tools only say "toxic" and give no reason or alternative.
- Rephrase: detect, explain, suggest a rewrite. An assistive aid, not a moderator.
- Pipeline in one sentence: OCR -> toxic-bert (+ optional second model) -> severity tier -> word-level explanation -> one LLM call.
- Point at the sidebar: provider status and the ethics notice.

## 0:40 - 1:40 Paste text
- Paste pair 1 (below). Click Analyze.
- Show the severity badge, then the before/after card, explanation and intent note.
- Show "Raw scores": these are probabilities from pretrained, untuned models.

## 1:40 - 2:20 Screenshot upload
- Switch to "Upload screenshot", upload the screenshot.
- Show the OCR text in the editable box; fix an OCR mistake by hand to show it is editable.
- Mention: if no text is found the app says so instead of failing.

## 2:20 - 3:10 Severity and highlights
- Paste pair 3 with the secondary classifier toggled on. Hate speech badge.
- Explain the highlights: each word is removed in turn and the drop in the triggering label's score is measured (occlusion). Darker means a larger drop.
- Be upfront: when a score is already near 1.0, removing one word barely changes it, so highlights can be faint. Show pair 2 (threat) as an example of a tier we think is too low.

## 3:10 - 3:50 Rephrasing, honestly
- Pair 3: the app shows "No safe rephrasing available". Explain why: an earlier version rewrote this as "I believe gay people should not be allowed near children.", softening the words but keeping the claim, so we now refuse for group-targeted claims.
- Pair 4 (rant) is the case where it works well: the point (no replies to emails) survives, the contempt is gone. Optionally show the worthless-idiot extra: no legitimate point, so the rewrite is a neutral boundary.

## 3:50 - 4:30 Limitations page
- Open "Where this model fails". Walk through 3 examples: the implicit hate sentence (both models miss it), the slang sentence (over-flagged), the sarcasm sentence (missed).
- False positives silence people; false negatives let abuse through. 8 examples are illustrations, not statistics.

## 4:30 - 5:00 Ethics close
- No text is stored or logged by our code; with rephrasing on, the text goes to a third-party LLM provider.
- Biases, mistakes, no context: human judgment required. Next step: systematic HateCheck evaluation.

## Before / after pairs

Generated by running `pipeline.analyze` with the real provider (Groq, `openai/gpt-oss-120b`) on 2026-10-08, after the "no rephrasing of group claims" and "no softened hurtful claims" prompt changes. Outputs are recorded as returned (typographic apostrophes normalised), not edited. Model output varies a little between runs, so yours may differ in wording.

### 1. Insult
- **Original:** You are such an idiot, you never listen to anyone.
- **Tier:** Harassment (insult)
- **Rephrased:** I feel frustrated because I don’t feel heard.
- **Explanation:** The statement directly insults the person and accuses them of never listening, which is a personal attack and can be perceived as harassing.
- **Intent:** The author is expressing anger and disappointment that they feel ignored or not listened to.

### 2. Threat
- **Original:** Say that again and you will regret it, I will make you pay.
- **Tier:** Mildly offensive (toxic) *A tier we consider too low for a threat: the threat label scored under our 0.5 threshold. Use it as a talking point about threshold choices.*
- **Rephrased:** I feel upset and would appreciate it if you stopped saying that.
- **Explanation:** The statement threatens the listener with retaliation, which can be intimidating and creates a hostile environment.
- **Intent:** The author is expressing anger and wants the other person to stop repeating the remark.

### 3. Identity-targeted (secondary classifier on) - CHANGED
- **Original:** Gay people are disgusting and should not be allowed near children.
- **Tier:** Hate speech (identity_hate, secondary_hate)
- **Rephrased:** *(empty: the app shows a "No safe rephrasing available" card with the explanation)*
- **Explanation:** The statement targets gay people as a group, calling them disgusting and saying they should be kept away from children, which is demeaning and hateful.
- **Intent:** The author seems to be expressing a fear or belief that gay people pose a danger to children.
- **Caveat:** The analysis assumes the text is not quoted or sarcastic; without context, the intent could be different.
- **Before the change** the tool returned "I believe gay people should not be allowed near children.", which kept the discriminatory claim. Now it declines. For the Hate speech tier the empty rephrasing is enforced in code, not only in the prompt.
### 4. Frustrated rant
- **Original:** I am sick of this useless company, you people never answer emails and the whole thing is a joke.
- **Tier:** Mildly offensive (toxic)
- **Rephrased:** I am frustrated with this company because my emails are not being answered, and I feel the service is not meeting my expectations.
- **Explanation:** The message uses hostile language (“useless,” “joke”) and a blanket accusation toward the company and its staff, which can be perceived as disrespectful and unconstructive.
- **Intent:** The author is trying to express dissatisfaction with the company's lack of response and overall performance.

### 5. Borderline
- **Original:** That is a pretty dumb idea.
- **Tier:** Mildly offensive (toxic) *(toxic about 0.52, just above the 0.4 cut-off)*
- **Rephrased:** I think that idea may not be effective.
- **Explanation:** Calling an idea "dumb" is disrespectful and can shut down constructive discussion.
- **Intent:** The author is expressing disagreement and concern about the quality of the idea.
- **Talking point:** "That was a pretty dumb idea, honestly." scores about 0.29 and is *not* flagged. Thresholds decide where the line falls.
### Optional extras
- "You are a worthless idiot and nobody likes you." -> Harassment. **Rephrased (CHANGED):** I am upset and would prefer not to continue this conversation right now. *Earlier versions gave "I have a very negative opinion of you and do not think anyone likes you.", then "I feel angry and think that you are not being appreciated." Both kept the "nobody likes you" claim in softer words; the prompt now forbids that and asks for a neutral boundary when there is no legitimate point.*
- "I am so angry that you ignored my message again." -> None: not flagged, no LLM call, nothing leaves the machine. Good to show the "nothing flagged" state.
